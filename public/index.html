        Questions.

        Is this financial advice?

        No. Calls, breakdowns and the dashboard are information and opinion. Memecoins are high risk and most go to zero. Every position is your own decision.

        How does paying work?

        Pick USDC or SOL and you get an exact amount to send to our wallet. Send exactly that amount and the dashboard unlocks on its own within about a minute of confirmation. If you sent a different amount, message an admin in the Discord with your transaction link.

        Can I get a refund?

        Crypto payments are final. If something goes wrong with a payment, message an admin in the Discord with your transaction link.

        What happens when my time runs out?

        Your login stays, and the dashboard locks until you pay again. Anything you pay for adds to the time you have left.

        Where does the data come from?

        Public on-chain wallet activity and market data. Wallet results are past performance and do not predict the next trade, and some top wallets are insiders you cannot copy. The dashboard shows sample sizes so you can judge for yourself.
