Penthouse Clipping Program

What is the Penthouse Clipping Program?

The Penthouse Clipping Program pays creators to turn Penthouse content into short, shareable clips. You post them on your own TikTok, X and Instagram accounts, and you earn from the views they get.

It helps Penthouse reach new traders, and it gives you a way to earn from content you already know how to make.

How does clipping work?

  1. Pick your source

    Choose from Penthouse streams, calls and breakdowns shared in the clipping channel on Discord.

  2. Cut the clip

    Find a strong moment, trim it short and format it vertical for TikTok, X and Instagram.

  3. Post and submit

    Post it on your own account, then submit the link in Discord so your views can be tracked.

Clip rules.

  • Post on your own account and keep the clip public for at least 30 days.
  • Use only content from the clipping channel. No made-up results or fake screenshots.
  • No promises of profit. Keep the “not financial advice” line in the caption.
  • Clips that mislead viewers are not paid and can remove you from the program.
