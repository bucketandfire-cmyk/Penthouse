The Penthouse Beginners Guide

A short series for people new to Solana memecoins. A new part is released each week. It explains the basics in plain words and shows where the dashboard fits. It is education, not financial advice.

Part 1: How a memecoin launch works.

A memecoin is a token with no product behind it. Its price comes only from how many people want to buy it right now. On Solana, most start on a launchpad such as pump.fun, where anyone can create a coin in a minute.

  • Launch. A creator (the dev) makes the coin and usually buys some first. Early buyers get the lowest price.
  • Bonding curve. On pump.fun the price rises as people buy along a fixed curve. When enough has been bought, the coin “migrates” to a normal trading pool.
  • Market cap. Price times supply. A small market cap can move a lot on a few buys, in both directions.
  • Most coins go to zero. A tiny number run. Plan around losing what you put in on any single coin.

How the dashboard helps. The Metas tab shows which themes are getting many new launches. The Devs tab shows creators whose past coins ran. Neither tells you what the next coin will do.

Rules to start with: only use money you can lose, size every trade small, never share your seed phrase, and treat every link in a DM as hostile.
